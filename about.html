ℹ️ ABOUT SCHOOL HUB

Learn Smarter. Prepare Better.

SCHOOL HUB is a digital learning platform designed to help students organize their study materials, practice questions and prepare effectively for examinations.

🎯 Our Mission

Why SCHOOL HUB exists

SCHOOL HUB aims to make quality study resources easier to access and easier to practice. Students can read notes, practice MCQs, take entrance mock exams, review difficult topics and track their learning progress in one organized place.


The platform is built with a focus on simplicity, accessibility and examination-oriented preparation.
